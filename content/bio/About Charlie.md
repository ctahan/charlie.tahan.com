## Current Bio

Dr. Charles Tahan is a Partner at Microsoft Quantum, where he leads technical teams working both internally and with Microsoft's close partners to build the world's first useful quantum computers. He is also a Visiting Research Professor in the Physics Department at the University of Maryland-College Park.

From 2020 to 2024, Dr. Tahan was Assistant Director for Quantum Information Science and Director of the National Quantum Coordination Office (NQCO) in the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy, where his office coordinated the more than 20 agencies of the National Quantum Initiative to develop and execute a national strategy to strengthen American leadership in quantum information science and technology. That role capped an almost 17-year career as a practicing physicist and technical leader at the National Security Agency and the Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency (DARPA), where he worked on quantum computing, high-performance computing, and other advanced technologies. At NSA's Laboratory for Physical Sciences (LPS) he served as Chief Scientist of Physical Sciences Research, Chief of the Solid State and Quantum Information Science Division, and Director of the LPS Qubit Collaboratory.

His contributions have been recognized by the Presidential Rank Award, the Presidential Early Career Award for Scientists and Engineers, election as a Fellow of the American Physical Society, NSA's Researcher of the Year award, and as an ODNI Science and Technology Fellow. Dr. Tahan earned a PhD in Physics at the University of Wisconsin-Madison in 2005 and a B.S. in Physics and Computer Science with Highest Honors from the College of William & Mary in 2000. From 2005 to 2007 he was a National Science Foundation Distinguished International Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the University of Cambridge.
