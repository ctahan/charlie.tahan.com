Two Presidential directives:

[National Security Memorandum 10 on Quantum Computing](https://bidenwhitehouse.archives.gov/briefing-room/statements-releases/2022/05/04/national-security-memorandum-on-promoting-united-states-leadership-in-quantum-computing-while-mitigating-risks-to-vulnerable-cryptographic-systems/) ([[web-archive/National Security Memorandum on Promoting United States Leadership in Quantum Computing While Mitigating Risks to Vulnerable Cryptographic Systems - The White House.pdf|saved copy]]) directing the Federal agencies to promote quantum R&D, workforce and education activities, and partnerships; move the Nation to quantum-resistant cryptography; and protect investments in QIST.

[Executive Order on Enhancing the National Quantum Initiative Advisory Committee](https://bidenwhitehouse.archives.gov/briefing-room/presidential-actions/2022/05/04/executive-order-on-enhancing-the-national-quantum-initiative-advisory-committee/) ([[web-archive/Executive Order on Enhancing the National Quantum Initiative Advisory Committee - The White House.pdf|saved copy]]) recommitting the U.S. to the National Quantum Initiative as a "substantial and sustained national priority" and relaunching the NQI Advisory Committee as a Presidential committee.

Augmenting the national quantum strategy:

[SCQIS Releases Strategic Plan for Bringing Quantum Sensors to Fruition](https://www.quantum.gov/scqis-releases-strategic-plan-for-bringing-quantum-sensors-to-fruition/) making policy recommendations to bring quantum sensors from lab to market and mission.

[QIST Workforce Development National Strategic Plan](https://www.quantum.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/QIST-Natl-Workforce-Plan.pdf) based on significant interagency work with policy recommendations: EDUCATE/INSPIRE/EXPERIENCES/CAREERS.

Increasing international cooperation:

[Entanglement Exchange Links Quantum Researchers Across Twelve Nations](https://entanglementexchange.org/entanglement-exchange-links-quantum-researchers-across-twelve-nations.html) launching a multi-nation clearinghouse for people exchange opportunities in QIST across our countries.

[The United States and France Sign Joint Statement to Enhance Cooperation on Quantum](https://www.quantum.gov/the-united-states-and-france-sign-joint-statement-to-enhance-cooperation-on-quantum/) signed during the state visit from President Macron to the U.S.

[The United States and Switzerland Sign Joint Statement to Strengthen Collaboration on Quantum](https://www.quantum.gov/the-united-states-and-switzerland-sign-joint-statement-to-strengthen-collaboration-on-quantum/) followed by Swiss-US Quantum Days in Chicago.

[The United States and Denmark Take Steps to Strengthen Quantum Cooperation](https://www.quantum.gov/the-united-states-and-denmark-take-steps-to-strengthen-quantum-cooperation/) preceded by the Neils Bohr Centennial Symposium, which I had the great pleasure to attend.

[Readout: International Roundtable on Pursuing Quantum Information Together: 2^N vs 2N](https://www.quantum.gov/readout-international-roundtable-2n/), a multi-lateral meeting we had at the White House in May to discuss shared values in QIST.

[The United States and Sweden Sign Quantum Cooperation Statement](https://www.quantum.gov/the-united-states-and-sweden-sign-quantum-cooperation-statement/)

[The United States and Finland Move to Strengthen Cooperation in Quantum](https://www.quantum.gov/the-united-states-and-finland-move-to-strengthen-cooperation-in-quantum/)

Quantum-relevant legislation:

[Quantum Computing Cybersecurity Preparedness Act](https://www.congress.gov/bill/117th-congress/house-bill/7535), a close companion to NSM-10.

[Quantum in the CHIPS and Science Act of 2022](https://www.quantum.gov/quantum-in-the-chips-and-science-act-of-2022/) creating opportunities for infrastructure investments relevant to quantum in the CHIPS Act and authorizing new programs for Quantum Networks, quantum user access, and the Q-12 program.

[The FY 2022 NDAA and Quantum](https://www.quantum.gov/the-fy-2022-ndaa-and-quantum/) legislating the NSTC Economic and Securities Implications of Quantum Science (ESIX) Subcommittee, among other activities.

A document prepared by the NQCO, showing the National Quantum Initiative Act with these amendments is available [here](https://www.quantum.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/NQIA2018-NDAA2022-CHIPS2022.pdf).
