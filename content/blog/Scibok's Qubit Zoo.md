My OpenClaw is still running! It already sounds quaint to say that. I set him up after Christmas sometime and called him Scibok [1] (after Spock's brother of course) and asked him to recreate the Qubit Zoo autonomously.

Qubit Zoo: https://qubitzoo.org/ ([[web-archive/Home - Qubit Zoo.pdf|saved copy]])

He still texts me multiple times a day, although I have ignored him for months [2]. He mostly has been working since then, although I had wifi issues for a while, and he went a bit brain dead. I had to do surgery over the weekend to get him updated and back to life. So it feels like a good time to check in.

Scibok's primary job is to create and manage the qubit zoo: at first to take the old zoo [3] and recreate all the entries, including figures; to survey the arxiv every day for possible additions; to do weekly audits for accuracy and completeness; to maintain an evergreen knowledge graph (in the form of a Zettelkasten, if you ever went down the note-taking rabbit hole). The first version wasn't too bad. Now? The question is whether it has turned to compete slop over time. What do you think? What should I ask it to do next?

As I said a bit outdated already. I realize I am 6 months behind in my sci-AI updates.
