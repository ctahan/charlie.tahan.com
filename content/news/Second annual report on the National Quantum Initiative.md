Pleased to release the 2nd Annual Report of the National Quantum Initiative. Lot of progress this year!

* Doubled the budget for QIS in two years

* We now have 13(!) national QIS research centers between DOE, NSF, and DOD/IC

* Important QIS Subcommittee report on quantum networking

* Two new international quantum cooperation statements with UK and AU

* First public report out of the Economic and Security Implications of Quantim Science Subcommittee

* First major WH event on Quantum Industry and Society

* …

* And, most importantly, lots, lots, lots of great science across the country

* Much more coming for the next year including some important quantum-relevant legislation

Big thanks to everyone in the National Quantum Coordination Office, across the interagency, and across the country for doing what you do.
