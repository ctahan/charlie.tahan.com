What an epic week for quantum in DC. I wanted to thank everyone for sending me photos by putting them in one place. It was an honor to be able to announce the new Microsoft quantum center with the Governor of Maryland at QWC2025. My thanks to the key people on both the Maryland and Microsoft sides that made it happen. And it was great to catch up with friends in the quantum community, old and new, from all over the world. I believe that small groups of people with common purpose can do amazing things. If we can bring them together in the right places and enable them with the right tools...we can change the (quantum) world.
