Today we released the first report from the NQI Advisory Committee. Please check it out if you are interested in the future of the NQI.

https://www.quantum.gov/nqiac-report-on-renewing-the-national-quantum-initiative/ ([[web-archive/NQIAC Report on Renewing the National Quantum Initiative - National Quantum Initiative.pdf|saved copy]])

I want to thank my co-chair Kathryn Moler, the co-chairs of our three subcommittees, Nadya Mason Will Oliver James Clarke Grace W. John Preskill Gilbert Herrera and members Krysta Svore Jamil Abo-Shaeer Jun Ye Deborah Frincke, PhD Mark Ritter Robert Schoelkopf Fred Chong for all their hard work over the last six months developing our recommendations for the renewal of the NQI Act.
