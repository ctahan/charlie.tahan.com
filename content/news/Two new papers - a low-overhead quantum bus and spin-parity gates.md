Two newly published papers from the group worth highlighting:

1) Low Overhead Quantum Bus with Coupling beyond the Nearest Neighbor via Mediated Effective Capacitance

From Yariv Yanay: using experience from our long-running work with MIT designing superconducting qubit arrays for quantum many-body physics…how to make new types of qubit couplers and buses without a lot of wires.

https://journals.aps.org/prapplied/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevApplied.20.024006

https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.04284

2) Quantum Computation by Spin Parity Measurements with Encoded Spin Qubits

From Matt Brooks: more progress on more efficient measurement-based gate-based quantum computing on encoded spin qubits…here explicitly showing how to use recently experimentally demonstrated spin-parity measurements to perform an encoded two qubit gate while minimizing leakage.

https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevB.108.035206

https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.09727

Both ideas worth trying out!
