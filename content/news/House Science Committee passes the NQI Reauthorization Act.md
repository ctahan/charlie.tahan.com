On Wednesday, the House Science Committee passed the National Quantum Initiative Reauthorization Act advancing this critical legislation toward an eventual House vote.

https://science.house.gov/2023/11/science-committee-passes-bills-to-secure-quantum-commercial-space-leadership

We greatly appreciate the leadership Chairman Lucas, Ranking Member Lofgren, and the entire Committee has shown in their support for the reauthorization and expansion of the NQI. There is a lot to like in this Act and we look forward to the bill getting even better as the House and Senate work to reauthorize the NQI.
